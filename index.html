Pilot, engineer, founder

I help organizations turn ideas into scalable technology by combining AI, cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity and software engineering.

I studied computer science engineering at the Escuela Militar de Aviación Marco Fidel Suárez and flew for the Colombian Air Force from 2009 to 2019: 1,500 flight hours, and a final post leading research, development and innovation in Barranquilla.

Alongside flying I started building for the web. Between 2017 and 2021 I freelanced and led three ventures as CEO or CTO: monkki, The FitMode and OUTIMPO. Since 2021 I've worked at Endeavor, first in Colombia and, since 2023, on the global team in New York. The cockpit habits came along: checklists, redundancy, and a calm voice on the radio when something breaks.

My work at Endeavor

Endeavor is the global community of high-impact entrepreneurs, building thriving entrepreneurial ecosystems in emerging and underserved markets. My job is the technology underneath it: AI systems, cloud infrastructure and security the whole network can depend on.

  1. Cloud infrastructure

    AWS and Linux servers: hosting, networking, backups and monitoring for Endeavor's web platforms.

  2. Identity and access

    Okta for single sign-on, multi-factor authentication and access policies, so the right people reach the right tools and nobody else does.

  3. Applications and data

    Salesforce administration, the WordPress sites, the learning management system and Endeavor Campus.

  4. People

    Entrepreneurs, mentors and staff. Every layer exists to protect their data and their time.

Bringing AI into the work

Building AI systems is half the job; the other half is helping people use them. At the 2026 Endeavor Global Retreat in New York I led the AI kickoff training for an in-person hackathon at our office, and I run hands-on AI sessions with Endeavor's teams.

The hardest part of adopting AI isn't the technology. It's us.

On the global team I also work as a product manager: shaping requirements, setting OKRs and getting changes shipped across teams.

athmira

Train smarter. Go further.

A training app I'm building for beginner and intermediate cyclists and triathletes. Its camera bike fit reads joint angles from the side, front and back with on-device pose detection, alongside tire pressure, fueling and aero estimates.

One Expo codebase runs the web app and the iOS and Android apps, backed by Supabase.

  • Expo and React Native
  • MediaPipe pose detection
  • TypeScript
  • Supabase
  • Vercel

KALMA Play Café

Crecer con Kalma. Vivir con Alma.

The website and booking platform for a play café for young children and their families in Pereira, Colombia. Families book play time with live availability and pay online, sign up for classes and memberships, and check in with a QR code and a digital signature.

The team runs bookings, the menu, the blog and a family CRM from its own panel, and Slow, the café's sloth mascot, answers questions before handing off to WhatsApp.
